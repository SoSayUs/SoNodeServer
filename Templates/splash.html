If you could run your own government as easily as using Facebook,

Would you?


Currently, participation in democracy means to check a box for one of two or three people, every four years. Many years ago when democracy was created, that was not the case.

Then, citizens could vote on laws and issues themselves, not others to make those decisions on their behalf. It failed partly because citizens either voted in ignorance or were unable to vote at all. Things are different today; almost everyone is able to learn with great convenience and vote from great distance.

Today, legislation consists of two chambers, The House and The Senate. The conditions have come to be for a third: The Citizenry. A chamber that consists of every Citizen and empowers them with a duty to collectively navigate a way forward.

As Citizens,

We say that reponsibly engaging with our government is part of our civic duty.

We say we can enable ourselves by unifiying our collective will.

We say if enough of us gather, we will be heard.

Here you will:

  • Represent yourself in all levels of government.
  • Debate the merits of any bill or issue.
  • Vote yea or nay on any bill.
  • Write your own bills.

  • Match your voting history to candidates to see who would best represent your will.
  • Reduce partisan politics by taking power from those who would vote along party lines.

  • Write all votes to an open source, decentralized blockchain.
  • Run a node in each and every locality by common citizens.

  • Decentralize Democracy.
